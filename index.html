The Story Behind 📖

In 2005, a young man named Alex Tew created "The Million Dollar Homepage" and sold one million pixels at $1 each. He made a million dollars!

Today, we revive this idea with an Arabic twist. Not just an ad site, but a digital museum documenting a moment in time.

Every cell tells a story. Every logo carries a dream. Once the page fills up, it becomes a permanent historical archive.
